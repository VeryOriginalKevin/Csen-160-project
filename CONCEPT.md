ive been playing a lot of backgammon. it feels too random! each turn, instead of randomly rolling 2 dice with fixed turn order, 4 dice are rolled! then, the 2 players compete in a short contest of skill! (a trivia question) The winner goes first for this turn order and gets to hand pick which 2 of the 4 dice they want. The loser goes last and is left with the last two

We are doing local multiplayer. the trivia has 2 sets of buttons, 1 for each player. this is the only time both players are acting at the same time. If someone answers the question right, they are immediately the only one in control of the game. They select their 2 dice from the 4 and then move. If someone answers wrong, the reverse happens.

The project will have one 1 application which displays the visual interface for both the trivia and the backgammon. It should be visually clean.
